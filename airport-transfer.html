FAQ

Marrakech airport transfer — common questions

Where do I meet the driver at Marrakech Airport?

Your chauffeur waits in the arrivals hall with a name board showing your name. After you collect luggage, they escort you to the parked vehicle outside the terminal.

Are prices per person or per vehicle?

All rates on this page are per vehicle, one-way, for the passenger band shown (1–4 or 5–8). The VIP Mercedes E-Class is a premium private sedan.

Do you cover delayed flights?

Yes. We monitor your flight and adjust pickup time if you land late — so you are not charged waiting fees for normal airport delays.

Can I book a transfer to Casablanca, Fès or Agadir?

Absolutely. Long-distance private transfers from Marrakech Airport to Casablanca, Agadir, Essaouira, Rabat, Fès, Meknes, Beni Mellal and Tanger are listed above with fixed EUR prices.
