Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

excursionmarrakech (“we”) collects information you submit when requesting or paying for an excursion: name, email, phone number (with country), travel date, party size, preferred display currency, and payment status via Stripe or PayPal when used.

What we use data for

  • Responding to booking inquiries and confirming trips (including WhatsApp/phone contact)
  • Processing payments through Stripe and/or PayPal
  • Improving our website and measuring advertising (Meta / Google) if configured

Cookies & ads

If Meta Pixel or Google Ads tags are enabled, they may set cookies to measure campaign performance. You can control cookies in your browser. We do not sell your personal data.

Payments

Card and PayPal details are processed by Stripe/PayPal. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

Analytics & advertising

If you accept cookies, we may use Google Analytics 4, Google Ads conversion tags and/or the Meta Pixel to understand traffic and measure booking inquiries. You can choose “Essential only” on the consent banner to refuse analytics and advertising cookies. Server booking data is still processed to fulfil your request.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your booking contact data by emailing us.
